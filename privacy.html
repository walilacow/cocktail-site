喵喵愛喝酒 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-11-22　最後更新：2026-11-22

一句話說明：喵喵愛喝酒不收集、不分享你的任何個人資料。你自己建立的酒譜、照片與收藏存在你的裝置上，我們看不到。

1. 我們不收集資料

本 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有我們自己的伺服器，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析、追蹤或當機回報工具。我們（小盜工作室）看不到你在 App 裡的任何內容。

2. 資料存在哪裡

你新增的自創酒譜、為酒譜拍攝或選擇的照片、你的收藏，存在你的裝置上。

這些資料不會同步到其他裝置，也不會傳送給我們或任何第三方。

單位偏好（毫升或盎司），以及你在首次開啟時完成成年確認的時間，只存在這台裝置上。我們不詢問、也不儲存你的生日或年齡。

刪除 App 會一併刪除這台裝置上的這些資料。若你的 iPhone 有開啟 iCloud 備份或以電腦備份，這些資料可能包含在你的裝置備份裡，由你自己的備份設定管理，我們無法存取。

3. 網路連線

App 內建的經典酒譜、名詞對照、知識文章與台灣酒吧、器材材料店家資料，都隨 App 一起安裝，瀏覽時不需要連網。

本 App 不會自行連網下載任何資料，店家資料隨 App 版本更新。

你點選店家的地圖、官方網站或「回報資料有誤」時，App 會分別打開 Apple 地圖、瀏覽器或郵件 App，之後的處理依各服務的政策。

4. App 會要求的權限

  • 相機：只在你為自創酒譜拍照時使用。照片存在你的裝置上（見第 2 節）。

從相簿選照片時，App 使用系統的照片選擇器：你在選擇器裡挑的那幾張才會交給 App，App 看不到你的其他照片，所以不需要「照片」權限。

不允許相機的話，自創酒譜仍可使用，也仍可從相簿選照片。本 App 不使用位置、麥克風、通訊錄或「健康」資料。

5. 第三方服務

本 App 不使用任何第三方 SDK 或第三方服務，也沒有廣告與 App 內購買。

6. 飲酒提醒與適用年齡

本 App 的內容與酒類有關，僅供已年滿十八歲者使用。禁止酒駕，飲酒過量有害健康，未滿十八歲禁止飲酒。

7. 兒童隱私

本 App 不適合未成年人使用，也不收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

8. 政策更新

如果之後的版本改變了資料處理方式，我們會先更新這一頁，並在 App 更新說明裡告知。

Bartender Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-22 · Last updated: 2026-11-22

In short: Bartender Toolkit does not collect or share any of your personal data. The recipes, photos and favorites you create are stored on your device, and we cannot see them.

1. We don't collect data

The app has no accounts, no servers of our own, and no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We (EngieStudio) cannot see anything you do in the app.

2. Where your data lives

Your own recipes, the photos you take or choose for them, and your favorites are stored on your device. They are not synced to other devices and are not sent to us or to any third party.

Your unit preference (ml or oz) and the time you confirmed your age on first launch are stored only on this device. We never ask for or store your birthday or age.

Deleting the app deletes this data from the device. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, the data may be included in that device backup, which you manage through your own backup settings; we have no access to it.

3. Network use

The built-in classic recipes, glossary, articles and Taiwan bar and supplier listings are installed with the app and need no network connection to browse.

The app does not connect to the internet by itself or download any data; listing updates arrive with app updates.

When you tap a venue's map, official website or “Report an error,” the app opens Apple Maps, your browser or your mail app, each governed by its own policy.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you take a photo for one of your own recipes. The photo is stored on your device (see section 2).

To add photos from your library, the app uses the system photo picker: only the photos you choose in the picker are handed to the app, and it cannot see the rest of your library, so no Photos permission is needed.

If you don't allow the camera, your own recipes still work and you can still add photos from your library. The app does not use location, the microphone, contacts or Health data.

5. Third-party services

The app uses no third-party SDKs or third-party services, and has no ads or in-app purchases.

6. Alcohol notice and intended age

This app contains alcohol-related content and is intended for adults only (age 18 and over). Never drink and drive, drinking too much is harmful to your health, drinking is prohibited under 18, and please drink responsibly.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not intended for minors and collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page first and mention it in the release notes.
