常見問題

酒譜的比例是從哪裡來的？

IBA（國際調酒師協會）公布的經典調酒，以 IBA 現行配方為準，另外再對照至少一個專業來源。其他常見經典也都核對過兩個以上來源。步驟、技巧與背景故事是我們自己撰寫的。本 App 與 IBA 無隸屬或贊助關係。

可以切換毫升和盎司嗎？

可以。在「更多 › 設定」或酒譜頁上切換。本 App 以 1 盎司＝30 毫升換算（吧台量酒器的慣例），盎司會取到最接近的 ¼（量很少時取 ⅛）。dash、吧匙、片、顆等單位不換算。

我自己的酒譜和照片會不見嗎？

你的自創酒譜、照片與收藏只存在這台裝置上，本版沒有同步功能。刪除 App 會一併刪除這些資料。

酒吧或店家的資料不對、已經歇業？

每家店都標有資料確認日期。資料超過一年沒有確認時，App 會提醒你可能已過時。請在店家頁按「回報資料有誤」寫信告訴我們，我們會查證後更新。店家資料內建在 App 裡，更新會隨 App 版本提供。

為什麼一打開要確認成年？

本 App 的內容與酒類有關，所以第一次開啟要請你確認已年滿十八歲。我們只記錄你按下確認的時間，不詢問也不儲存生日或年齡。

需要付費嗎？

不需要。本 App 完全免費，沒有廣告，也沒有 App 內購買。

FAQ

Where do the recipe ratios come from?

Classic cocktails published by the IBA (International Bartenders Association) follow the current IBA specs and are cross-checked with at least one other professional source. Other common classics are checked against two or more sources. Steps, tips and background stories are written by us. This app is not affiliated with or sponsored by the IBA.

Can I switch between ml and oz?

Yes, in More › Settings or on a recipe page. The app uses 1 oz = 30 ml (the bar jigger convention) and rounds ounces to the nearest ¼ (⅛ for very small amounts). Dashes, bar spoons, slices and similar units are not converted.

Will I lose my own recipes and photos?

Your own recipes, photos and favorites are stored only on this device, and this version has no sync feature. Deleting the app deletes them.

A bar or shop listing is wrong or closed.

Each listing shows the date it was last checked, and the app warns you when it hasn't been checked for over a year. Tap “Report an error” on the listing to email us; we'll verify it and update the listing. Listing data is built into the app, and updates arrive with app updates.

Why do I have to confirm my age?

The app contains alcohol-related content and is for adults aged 18 and over, so it asks you to confirm your age the first time you open it. We only record when you tapped confirm; we never ask for or store your birthday or age.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
